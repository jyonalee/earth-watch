Methods, sources, and limitations

How aggregates are built and what they do not claim.

Datasets

Canonical URLs and licenses are listed in docs/sources.md. Update frequency is typically monthly or annual per provider; the Worker runs on a weekly cron.

  • Chart (a): NASA GISTEMP v4, Met Office HadCRUT5.1.0.0, Berkeley Earth land+ocean (HadSST4).
  • Chart (b): Global Carbon Budget 2025 (pinned download), NOAA GML global CO₂ growth.
  • Chart (c): NOAA NCEI global ocean heat content (0–700 m, 0–2000 m ASCII), NOAA Climate at a Glance global ocean SST JSON.

Pinned GCB and HadCRUT paths are bumped manually — see docs/maintenance.md.

Baselines

  • Chart (a): common 1951–1980 anomaly baseline (native baselines rebased when needed).
  • Chart (c) SST: 1901–2000 (NCEI Climate at a Glance).

Trends and uncertainty

Linear trends use ordinary least squares with AR(1)-adjusted 95% confidence intervals (effective n from autocorrelation of residuals). Trend bands on charts show uncertainty in the fitted trend, not year-to-year scatter.

Complete calendar years only: the current UTC year is excluded from trends and “last complete year” labels when monthly coverage is incomplete (HadCRUT, GISTEMP, Berkeley, GCB/GML alignment).

Airborne fraction (chart b)

Airborne fraction = atmospheric CO₂ growth (GtC) ÷ (fossil + land-use emissions), using NOAA GML global growth and GCB emissions. CO₂ ppm is converted with 2.124 GtC per ppm (cited in the chart JSON).

Ocean heating rate (chart c)

OHC trend slope (10²² J yr⁻¹) is converted to a global mean heating rate in W m⁻² using Earth’s surface area 5.10072×10¹⁴ m² and a tropical year in seconds (see chart JSON citation).

Known limitations

  • SST is not OISST: chart (c) uses the official NCEI Climate at a Glance global ocean index, not a gridded OISST area mean (NetCDF is not processed in the Worker).
  • Berkeley Earth can lag other temperature products by a few months.
  • Land-use emissions uncertainty dominates airborne-fraction error bars.
  • Health: external monitors should poll /api/health; the Worker does not send alerts.
